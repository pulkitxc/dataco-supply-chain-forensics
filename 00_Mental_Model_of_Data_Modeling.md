\# Business Reality Mental Model



A database is not a collection of tables.



It is a structured representation of business reality.



Data modeling is the process of translating business reality into a structure that a computer can store without losing its meaning.



SQL is then used to question that representation.



The answers generated from SQL help decision-makers understand the business and make better decisions.
